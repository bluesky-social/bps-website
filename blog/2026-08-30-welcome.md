Bluesky Protocol Services manages infrastructure built on [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com). This includes the [Bluesky API](https://bsky.network/docs/bluesky-api), the [firehose](https://bsky.network/docs/relay) of public content on the network, and the [Jetstream](https://bsky.network/docs/jetstream) for filtering the firehose.

This blog is where we'll announce new features, post operational updates, and let you know about any deprecations. Expect the volume to be pretty low.

{/* truncate */}

Bluesky product announcements will continue to happen on the [company blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog) and writing about the protocol will likewise continue on the [atproto blog](https://atproto.com/blog).

BPS is its own thing and deserves a place for announcements.

## Subscribing
Follow [bsky.network on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/bsky.network) for updates. 

Alternatively, subscribe to the [RSS](pathname:///blog/rss.xml),
[Atom](pathname:///blog/atom.xml), and/or [JSON](pathname:///blog/feed.json)
feeds.

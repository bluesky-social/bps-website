Protocol Services runs the infrastructure the network leans on — the relay,
Jetstream, and the APIs in these docs. Until now, changes to it were announced
wherever there was room: a docs edit, a GitHub thread, a post. None of those
are a record you can subscribe to.

This is that record.

{/* truncate */}

## What lands here

Operational news about the services documented on this site: capacity and
endpoint changes, deprecations and their timelines, new features in Jetstream
and the relay, and postmortems when we owe you one.

Protocol-level writing — lexicon design, specification work, the shape of AT
Protocol itself — stays on [the atproto.com blog](https://atproto.com/blog),
which is also where every post published before this one lives.

## Subscribing

The blog publishes [RSS](pathname:///blog/rss.xml),
[Atom](pathname:///blog/atom.xml), and [JSON](pathname:///blog/feed.json)
feeds. Point a reader at any of them.
